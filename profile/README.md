## Towards democratization of plant research.

DataPLANT’s mission is to lead the digital transformation in plant science by advancing from traditional publications to innovative data-driven formats like Annotated Research Contexts (ARC).

A central dogma of DataPLANT is to **learn from and apply software development principles** to achieve this mission.
In this spirit, you'll find here our official GiHub organization that contains our tools, services, knowledgebase, and community content.

If you are familiar with GitHub, you will most likely find the repository you want to contribute to below.
If that is not the case, check out our introduction to [what we are doing on GitHub]() first.
